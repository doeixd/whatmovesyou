# The four natures (summarized from *The Edge Effect*)

Braverman links each neurotransmitter to a brain region, a brain wave type and a personality type. These are the book's claims, summarized. They are not established science (see [validity.md](validity.md)).

## Dopamine: "brain power" (~17%)
- **Balanced:** strong-willed, rational, strategic and confident. Comfortable with facts more than feelings. Works well under stress and needs little sleep.
- **Typical roles:** doctors, scientists, engineers, inventors, generals, architects.
- **Excess:** risk-taking, impulsivity, controlling others, aggression, high sex drive that strains relationships.
- **Deficiency signs:** low energy, fatigue, sluggishness, memory loss, "the blues," sugar/carb cravings, low libido, poor concentration, procrastination.

## Acetylcholine: "brain speed"
- **Balanced:** creative, intuitive, open to new ideas, social and charismatic, romantic and altruistic. Enjoys words, ideas and communication.
- **Typical roles:** counselors, mediators, artists, writers, actors, advertising, yoga instructors, clergy, public service.
- **Excess:** gives too much of oneself, feels taken advantage of, paranoia, social isolation, panic, anxiety.
- **Deficiency signs:** forgetfulness, slower processing, patchy recall, reduced creativity, fat cravings, dry mouth, loss of muscle tone.

## GABA: "brain rhythm" (~50%)
- **Balanced:** stable, calm, organized, punctual and loyal. A team player who likes routine, tradition and planning.
- **Typical roles:** administrators, accountants, nurses, EMTs, air-traffic controllers, security staff, homemakers.
- **Excess:** over-nurturing, neglects own needs, relies heavily on partners and authority figures.
- **Deficiency signs:** anxiety, nervousness, irritability, tremors, palpitations, muscle tension, headaches, mood swings, impulsivity.

## Serotonin: "brain synchrony" (~17%)
- **Balanced:** lives in the moment, realistic, sensory, adventurous, good with their hands, resourceful in a crisis, the life of the party. Dislikes routine.
- **Typical roles:** tradespeople, pilots, drivers, military, surgeons, athletes, performers, detectives, turnaround executives.
- **Excess:** nervousness, hesitancy, fear of criticism, painful shyness.
- **Deficiency signs:** poor sleep (insomnia, early waking, night sweats), salt cravings, sadness, anxiety, rumination, loss of meaning.

## Book recommendations

For each deficiency, the book recommends diet changes, lifestyle changes and supplement doses scaled to the severity band. Examples: tyrosine/phenylalanine for dopamine, choline/phosphatidylcholine for acetylcholine, inositol/GABA/valerian/passionflower for GABA, and 5-HTP/tryptophan for serotonin.

The dose tables in the source PDFs didn't come through text extraction cleanly, so they are **not** transcribed here. Check the original book for them, and don't use them without a clinician. 5-HTP, tryptophan and St. John's Wort interact dangerously with SSRIs and other serotonergic drugs.

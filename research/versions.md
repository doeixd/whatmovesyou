## The "six extra questions" don't exist

I compared the bravermantest.com question data item by item against the 315-item book version. **It adds no new questions.** Instead, it has:

- **Fewer items:**
  - The free page drops "I'm obsessed with my deficiencies" from 2B, leaving 24 items.
  - The paid sample report also drops "I usually do not forget a face" from 2A.
- **Small wording changes, with the same meaning:**
  - "physical altercations" → "fights"
  - "how many people" → "how people"
  - "low cholesterol" → "really low cholesterol"
  - "change" → "change things"
  - "visions" → "vision"
  - "improve their morals" → "improve upon their morals"
  - "handy" → "very handy"
  - "friends' birthdays" → "friend's birthday"
  - "anyone's stories but mine" → "... but my own"
  - "lost many friends" → "lost my friends"
- **One changed item:** 4A "I have very low blood pressure" became "I have low blood pressure". That makes it the same as the 3A GABA item, so the statement now counts toward two natures.
- **Typos that change the meaning:**
  - 1B "I have trouble getting out of bed" is printed as "I have **little** trouble getting out of bed", which reverses it.
  - "I overea" is a cut-off "I overeat".
  - "alcohol or addiction" is missing "drug".
- **Different order:** the site asks the Character section before Personality.

The 321 figure is probably a miscount or marketing copy rather than a different edition. Braverman's later books (*Younger You*, 2007; *Younger Brain, Sharper Mind*, 2011) use the same four-neurotransmitter framework. I haven't checked whether they revise the questionnaire.
